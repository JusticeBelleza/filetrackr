# Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy for FileTrackr

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026

### 1. Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) outlines the mandatory security practices and acceptable use standards for all personnel accessing **FileTrackr**. Because this system handles official routing logs and administrative metadata for the Abra Provincial Health Office (PHO), strict adherence to these rules is required to protect government data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

### 2. Scope
This policy applies to all authorized users, including regular staff, clerks, department heads, and system administrators across the Abra PHO and its associated facilities:
*   Abra Provincial Hospital
*   La Paz District Hospital
*   Villaviciosa Medicare and Community Hospital
*   Dolores Medicare and Community Hospital
*   Bucay Municipal Hospital

### 3. Account and Credential Security
Your FileTrackr account is tied to your official digital identity. You are strictly responsible for all actions taken under your account.
*   **No Sharing:** You must never share your password, passkey, or biometric authentication device with anyone, including your direct supervisor or ICT personnel. 
*   **Session Management:** You must explicitly log out of FileTrackr or lock your workstation when stepping away from your desk, especially in shared office environments.
*   **Authentication Verification:** Do not attempt to bypass or automate the Cloudflare Turnstile security checks presented during the login process.

### 4. Data Handling and Confidentiality
*   **Need-to-Know Basis:** You may only access, view, or route documents that are relevant to your official duties and department. 
*   **No Unauthorized Duplication:** Capturing screenshots, taking photographs of your monitor, or exporting routing logs for personal use or unauthorized distribution is strictly prohibited.
*   **Verification of Physical Custody:** When clicking "Receive" or "Forward" in the system, you must ensure you have physical possession of the corresponding document. Falsifying the digital location of a document is an administrative offense.

### 5. Prohibited Activities
Under no circumstances shall users engage in the following activities on FileTrackr:
*   **Privilege Escalation:** Attempting to manipulate the system's Row Level Security (RLS) to gain administrative privileges or access restricted departmental data.
*   **Impersonation:** Logging in using another employee's credentials to process documents on their behalf, even if they explicitly asked you to do so.
*   **System Disruption:** Introducing malware, executing automated scripts (scraping), or performing actions designed to slow down or crash the database infrastructure.
*   **Non-Official Use:** Utilizing the tracking system to process personal documents, non-government correspondence, or unofficial business.

### 6. Incident Reporting
Security is a shared responsibility. You must immediately report any of the following to the ICT Officer Designate:
*   Suspicion that your password has been compromised.
*   Noticing unauthorized routing actions logged under your name.
*   Discovering a vulnerability or error that allows you to see documents you shouldn't have access to.

### 7. Policy Enforcement and Disciplinary Action
System activity is actively logged and audited. Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy may result in:
*   Immediate suspension of your FileTrackr account.
*   Administrative disciplinary action in accordance with Civil Service Commission (CSC) rules and internal Abra PHO regulations.
*   Where applicable, legal action under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) or the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (RA 10175).
