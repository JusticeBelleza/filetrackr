# Terms and Conditions of Use for FileTrackr

**Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

These Terms and Conditions govern the access to and use of FileTrackr, an official digital administrative service of the Abra Provincial Health Office. They establish the rules and responsibilities applicable to users of the System and shall be read together with applicable civil service laws, internal administrative issuances, and the System's Privacy Policy.

#### 1. Purpose and Authority
FileTrackr is an internal electronic document tracking and routing system operated by the Abra Provincial Health Office. Its primary purpose is to establish a transparent, accountable, and highly verifiable chain of custody for official agency documents and communications. The operation of this system supports the agency's mandate for efficient public administration, official records management, and operational transparency.

#### 2. Scope of the Terms
These Terms apply exclusively to authorized internal users, including:
*   Employed personnel and officials of the Abra Provincial Health Office, including the following District, Municipal, and Medicare Hospitals:
    *   Abra Provincial Hospital
    *   La Paz District Hospital
    *   Villaviciosa Medicare and Community Hospital
    *   Dolores Medicare and Community Hospital
    *   Bucay Municipal Hospital
*   System administrators and designated ICT staff.
*   Authorized clerks and document custodians operating within the system's approved workflows.
*   **External / Inter-Agency Access:** Use of the System by offices, departments, or agencies outside the Abra Provincial Health Office hospital network is strictly conditional upon the execution and approval of a formal **Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)** and **Data Sharing Agreement (DSA)** with the Abra Provincial Health Office.

Public access to the system backend or administrative dashboards is strictly prohibited.

#### 3. Access and Account Registration
*   **Eligibility:** Access is restricted to active personnel of the Abra Provincial Health Office and its associated district hospitals. 
*   **Account Creation:** Accounts are provisioned centrally by the system administrators. Self-registration by unauthorized external parties is disabled.
*   **Credential Security:** Users are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their passwords and passkeys. 
*   **Account Deactivation:** Access will be immediately suspended or deactivated upon an employee's separation from the agency, transfer to an ineligible department, or upon administrative order.

#### 4. Acceptable and Prohibited Use
FileTrackr is strictly for official government business. Users are prohibited from:
*   Attempting to bypass or manipulate Row Level Security (RLS) policies to access documents outside their department or clearance level.
*   Using another employee's account or sharing login credentials.
*   Falsifying document tracking data, backdating routing logs, or submitting fraudulent administrative records.
*   Introducing malicious software, performing automated scraping, or interfering with system availability.
*   Using the platform for personal storage, unauthorized communications, or unlawful activities.

#### 5. Government Information and Official Records
*   **Restricted Information:** All routing logs, tracking metrics, and document metadata within FileTrackr are classified as internal government records. They are not for public distribution unless explicitly released through official Freedom of Information (FOI) procedures.
*   **Official Evidence:** System-generated timestamps and user audit logs are considered official, legally binding records of a document's custody and routing history. 
*   **Reference Numbers:** Users must utilize the system-generated tracking IDs when inquiring about a document's status.

#### 6. Accuracy of Information
Users are administratively responsible for ensuring that the actions they log (e.g., "Received," "Forwarded") accurately reflect the physical reality of the document's location. The Abra Provincial Health Office assumes no liability for administrative delays caused by user input errors or failure to update tracking statuses promptly.

#### 7. Privacy and Personal Information
The system processes personnel data (names, roles, login logs) strictly to maintain administrative accountability. Pursuant to the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, the Abra Provincial Health Office operates as the Personal Information Controller (PIC) for administrative personnel accounts and official health records processed within the System. For a comprehensive breakdown of what data is collected, your rights, and contact details for the Data Protection Officer, please refer to the **FileTrackr Privacy Policy**.

#### 8. System Availability
The Abra Provincial Health Office ICT office will endeavor to ensure continuous system availability during standard government operating hours. However, access may be temporarily suspended due to:
*   Scheduled maintenance and system upgrades.
*   Network interruptions or ISP outages.
*   Emergency security patching or incident response.

#### 9. Security Responsibilities
The agency utilizes advanced security safeguards, including encrypted database connections and bot-mitigation challenges. Users must actively participate in system security by utilizing secure local devices, reporting suspicious account activity immediately, and ensuring they log out of shared terminals.

#### 10. Intellectual Property
The FileTrackr system architecture, source code, database schemas, and user interface workflows are the proprietary intellectual property of the Abra Provincial Health Office. Users and connected units are granted a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access the platform. Decompilation, code extraction, unauthorized reproduction, or unauthorized third-party deployment without prior written authorization from the Provincial Health Officer is strictly prohibited. While the system tracking data constitutes government records subject to applicable public disclosure laws, the underlying proprietary code and security infrastructure may not be reverse-engineered, copied, or distributed.

#### 11. Third-Party Services
FileTrackr relies on enterprise-grade third-party infrastructure to function, including:
*   **Supabase / AWS:** For secure cloud database hosting and data storage in the `ap-northeast-2` region.
*   **Cloudflare:** For network routing and Turnstile security verification.

These providers process encrypted system data strictly on behalf of the Abra Provincial Health Office and are bound by stringent enterprise data processing agreements.

#### 12. Electronic Transactions and Logs
*   **Electronic Acknowledgment:** Clicking "Receive" or "Forward" within the system constitutes a formal electronic acknowledgment of document custody.
*   **Timestamps:** The exact server time recorded at the moment of submission serves as the official timestamp of the transaction for compliance and auditing purposes.
*   **Digital Signatures & Passkeys:** Captured digital signatures and biometric authentications performed within the system are recognized as legally binding electronic signatures pursuant to the **Electronic Commerce Act of 2000 (Republic Act No. 8792)** and the Rules on Electronic Evidence. Affixing a signature on the finalization pad confirms official receipt and custody of the underlying physical document.

#### 13. Suspension and Termination
System access may be administratively suspended or revoked prior to an employee's departure under the following circumstances:
*   Detection of unauthorized access attempts or suspicious login behavior.
*   Documented violations of these Terms of Use or the agency's Acceptable Use Policy.
*   Pending administrative investigations requiring the preservation of audit logs.

#### 14. Complaints and Assistance
For technical support, password resets, or to report system errors, users must contact the ICT Officer Designate. Disputes regarding document routing timelines or missing files should be directed to the respective department heads or the PESU Program Coordinator.

#### 15. Changes to the Terms
The Abra Provincial Health Office reserves the right to amend these Terms. Material changes to system rules will be communicated via administrative memorandum or an in-app notification upon your next login.
